## Under-served Categories & Opportunity Verdict
- **Perpetuals:** Dominated by Jupiter (63M+). New CLOBs face high hurdle. If building perps, focus on *non-dominant* asset classes or cross-chain integration.
- **Under-served:** 
    1. **Institutional/Real-World Yield:** High-utility stablecoins and tokenized assets (BUIDL, USDGO) are growing faster than general retail protocols.
    2. **Leveraged Staking/Restaking:** Liquid staking is huge (34M), but restaking relative to ETH ecosystem is early.
    3. **Hybrid CEX/DEX Liquidity:** CEXs still dominate deep liquidity; bridging retail from CEXs to chain remains inefficient.

**Verdict:** Do NOT build another general-purpose CLOB perps DEX. Build for yield-bearing assets or B2B/On-ramp infrastructure.
