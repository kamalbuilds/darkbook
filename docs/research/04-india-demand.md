## 2. Indian Regulatory Reality
- **30% VDA Tax:** Prevents institutional DeFi; forces retail to stablecoin savings only.
- **1% TDS:** High friction for high-frequency trading (perps/arbitrage).
- **UPI Friction:** Ramp/off-ramp is highly monitored. Most usage happens via P2P.

## 3. Indian User Pain Points
| Pain | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tax Reporting** | Extremely high burden for retail; discourages activity. |
| **Ramp In/Out** | UPI monitoring; reliance on P2P which is prone to freezing. |
| **Stablecoin/Savings** | Need for inflation-hedged yields. |
