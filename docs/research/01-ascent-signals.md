# Ascent DeFi Residency - Selection Rubric & Fit Assessment

## Selection Rubric (Inferred)
Superteam India programs (Ascent, Build Station, Solana Founders Villa) generally prioritize high-velocity builders with demonstrated execution capability.

*   **Proof of Work > Potential:** Applications must show code, deployed contracts, or functional UI. Ideation-phase projects are generally rejected.
*   **Active Links:** All work must be verifiable on-chain (Solana devnet/mainnet transactions) or via live hosted apps.
*   **Founder Commitment:** Preference for full-time building.
*   **Mechanism Design:** Specifically for DeFi, the rubric requires clear protocol logic, understanding of market structure (liquidity, slippage, trade-offs), and security considerations.
*   **GTM Velocity:** The focus on "30-day GTM experiment" signals that they select for teams prepared to launch/iterate immediately, not those stuck in endless protocol research.
*   **Community Fit:** Direct alignment with Solana DeFi ecosystem needs (orders of magnitude lower latency, capital efficiency, native primitives).

## Fit Assessment for Early-stage Perps CLOB (Size-privacy focus, 0 users, Devnet-only)

**Verdict: POOR FIT.**

*   **Why it misses the mark:** With zero users and devnet-only status, this project lacks "proof of work" and "active links" (traction). The residency explicitly asks for teams ready to execute a 30-day GTM experiment.
*   **Strategic misalignment:** A devnet perps CLOB is a high-stakes, infrastructure-heavy project. Without live users or significant volume, there is not enough "market structure" to diagnose.
*   **Better candidates:** They are likely looking for teams with some volume (even if low) who need to refine security, polish UI for mainstream users, or optimize market structure. A project stuck in internal protocol R&D (size-privacy infra) is likely better served by a pure infrastructure grant or a longer-term incubation program.
*   **Pivot to qualify:** Unless the project can immediately demonstrate live trading volume or a partner-driven launch within the 7-day window, it will likely be filtered out by residents who already have traction.
